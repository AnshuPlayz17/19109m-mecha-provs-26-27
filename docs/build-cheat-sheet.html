Build cheat sheet

Keep this on the build table. Each part has a drawing and a checklist. Port numbers come later, once the wiring is done. Fill in the last table by hand as you build, then send it to Claude for the code.

88 W is the limit. No more motors. No spare port either, so if one breaks, something has to come off.

02Size limits

  • Folded, nothing sticks up past the base stage: 17.75" tall, 0.25" under 18".
  • Nothing pokes out of the 18" cube at the start: toggle arm, balancers, claw funnels, tank fittings, wires.
  • All the way up, nothing reaches 50". The claw tops out at 47.75", so with the arm flip up, nothing on the claw can stick up more than ~2.25" above it.
  • Check the start size with a real 18" box before every event.

03Drivetrain

  • Track width 12" (24 holes) wheel center to wheel center. Narrower tips with the cascade up.
  • Gap between the inner rails about 10": the cascade (about 9.5") has to fit with ~0.25" each side.
  • Middle wheel shifted back, under the cascade's weight. Keep a gap so the wheels don't rub each other.
  • Stagger the left and right motors along the length so they don't hit in the middle.
  • No slop: every axle in bearings on both rails, shaft collars tight, no wobble when you shake a wheel.
  • Spin each side by hand: it should roll freely and the same as the other side.
  • Battery and drive motors as low as they go.

05Tracking wheels

  • Wheel stays on the floor when the robot rocks or a wheel lifts.
  • Sensor directly on the wheel's axle: no gears, no slop.
  • Pivot doesn't wobble side to side.
  • Push the robot one tile (23.8") by hand: the check screen should read close to 23.8.

06Cascade + balancers

  • 3 stages of 35 holes, 5-hole overlap: 30" of travel, top of claw at 47.75".
  • Brace every stage. 2.5" overlap is the least that works, so no wobble allowed.
  • Hardstops at the bottom and top. The cascade starts every match all the way down (the code zeroes there).
  • Rotation sensor on the spool axle, so it counts exactly what the spool does.
  • Balancers: a piston pushes them out by itself when the cascade is near the top, and they come back in when it drops. They must fold inside the 18" cube.
  • Bands or springs on the balancers so they're fully in when the air is off.

07PTO (3rd motor: cascade or intake)

  • With no air, it must sit on the cascade. If the air runs out, the lift still has all 3 motors.
  • The gear slides fully onto one side or the other, never half-meshed.
  • The code stops motor 3 before it shifts. Shifting takes about 0.15 s.
  • The other 2 cascade motors hold the height while the intake runs.

08Air

supply tube (any length) solenoid → piston (as short as possible)
  • Fill valve → Tank A → Tank B → gauge → the 4 solenoids. Pump to the game manual's limit.
  • Each solenoid right next to its piston, so the solenoid-to-piston tube is a few inches.
  • Single-acting pistons (claw, PTO, balancers): plug the second solenoid port; bands return them.
  • Gauge where you can read it from outside the robot.
  • Soapy water on every fitting while it's full: bubbles mean a leak.
  • Bench test: a full skills run of piston moves. Note the pressure at the end.
Decide the claw: the code has air closes the claw, bands open it. The old air diagram had it the other way. Pick one and tell Claude.

09Wiring and consistency

  • Label both ends of every cable with what it is (ports come later).
  • Strain relief near every plug: zip-tie the cable so a tug never pulls the plug.
  • No cable near a gear, chain, wheel or sharp edge; none crosses the cascade's path.
  • Leave slack on cables that ride up the cascade, and run them so they can't snag.
  • Battery swaps in under 10 s without touching other cables.
  • Brain screen and controller port easy to reach.
  • Starting jig: a block that puts the robot in the same spot every time.
  • Funnels on the claw: it grabs a pin even when it's off by an inch.
  • Hardstops on everything that moves: toggle arm, arm flip, claw, cascade.
  • Toggle arm rests on its hardstop at the start (the code zeroes there).
  • No slop anywhere in the drive or tracking wheels.
  • Pre-auto check screen all green before every match.
